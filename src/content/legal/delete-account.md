# Delete your account and data

Effective date: November 2, 2026

You can delete your DesiDots account and the personal data connected to it at any time.

## Option 1: in the app (fastest)
1. Open the DesiDots app and sign in.
2. Go to **Profile**.
3. Tap **Delete my account** (at the bottom).
4. Read the notice, type **DELETE** and confirm.

## Option 2: by email
If you cannot use the app, email **privacy@desidots.com** from the email address of your account with the subject "Delete my account". We may ask you to confirm it is you. We act on valid requests within 45 days.

## What happens
- Your account is **locked straight away** and you are signed out on every device.
- You have **30 days to change your mind**. Sign in again and choose "Keep my account".
- After 30 days it is **deleted for good** and cannot be restored.

## What is deleted
Your sign-in, profile (name, phone, photo and the details you added), saved addresses, cart, notifications, reviews, and any screenshots you sent to support.

## What we keep, and why
Records of **orders and payments** are kept for **7 years** because tax, accounting and refund rules require it. They are **separated from you**: your name, phone number and street address are removed and only the city and state remain. They are no longer linked to your account.

## When you cannot delete yet
If you have an order that is on its way, or a refund that is still being paid, finish or wait for it first. The app tells you. Seller and administrator accounts are closed by DesiDots: write to support@desidots.com.
