# Privacy Policy

**DRAFT: have a lawyer review before publishing.**
Effective date: November 2, 2026

**Groccy Technologies, Inc., doing business as DesiDots** ("DesiDots", "we") runs the DesiDots app and website. This policy explains what we collect, why, who we share it with, how long we keep it, and your choices. Questions: privacy@desidots.com.

## 1. What we collect
**Information you give us**
- **Account:** name, email address and profile photo from your Google account when you sign in (we never see your Google password).
- **Profile:** phone number, and optional details you add during setup (such as gender, date of birth and community) so we can personalise events and offers.
- **Delivery addresses:** name, phone, street, city, state, ZIP code and delivery notes.
- **Orders and tickets:** what you bought, from which store, the amount, status and tracking details.
- **Reviews and reports:** ratings, comments, and any screenshot or message you send to support.
- **Seller (vendor) information:** for sellers only, business details, tax and identity information needed for payouts (for example EIN, PAN, GSTIN, import-export code) and bank details for payouts. Full bank account numbers are stored separately with extra protection.

**Information collected automatically**
- Notification messages we create for your orders and payments, and whether you read them.
- Technical information needed to keep you signed in and secure (such as a session token).

**What we do not collect:** your card number (Stripe collects it on its own page); your device's GPS location (the app uses the addresses you type); advertising identifiers; we do not run third-party ad trackers.

## 2. Why we use it
- To create your account, show stores and events, and take and deliver your orders.
- To process payments, refunds and seller payouts.
- To send you messages about your orders, payments, refunds, and new events in your city.
- To prevent fraud and abuse and keep the Service secure.
- To meet legal, tax and accounting duties.
- To answer support requests and improve the Service.

We **do not sell** your personal information. We do not share it for advertising.

## 4. How long we keep it
- Account data: until you delete your account (see section 6).
- Orders and payments: kept for as long as the law and accounting require ([DECIDE: for example 7 years]), with your name, phone and street address removed when your account is deleted.
- Seller records: while the seller account is active and as required for tax and legal reasons afterwards.
- Support screenshots: deleted with your account.

## 5. Security
Data is sent over encrypted connections. Access to data is restricted by account and role. Sellers can see only their own orders; customers can see only their own data. Sensitive seller bank details are stored apart from other data and every time an administrator views a full account number it is logged. No system is perfectly secure; if there is a breach that affects you we will tell you as the law requires.

## 6. Your choices and rights
- **See or correct** your profile and addresses in the app (Profile).
- **Delete your account** in the app: Profile, "Delete my account". Your account locks immediately and your data is deleted for good after 30 days. Signing in again within the 30 days lets you keep the account. You cannot delete while an order is on its way or a refund is pending. Details: [Delete your account](/delete-account/).
- **Turn off notifications** in Profile.
- **Ask us** to access, correct, or delete your data, or to stop certain uses, by emailing privacy@desidots.com. We reply within 30 days (45 where the law allows).
- **California and other US state residents:** you may have the right to know what we collect, to delete it, to correct it, and not to be discriminated against for using these rights. We do not sell or share personal information for advertising.
- If you are outside the USA, local laws may give you more rights; contact us.

## 7. Children
The Service is for people **[DECIDE: 18]** and older. We do not knowingly collect information from children. If you think a child has given us information, contact privacy@desidots.com and we will delete it.

## 8. International transfers
Our service providers may process data in the United States and other countries. Orders from India involve sharing delivery details with sellers in India so they can ship to you.

## 9. Changes
We will update this page when our practices change and show a new date at the top. For important changes we will tell you in the app.
