# Privacy Policy

Effective date: November 2, 2026

**DesiDots** ("we", "us") runs the DesiDots app and website at www.desidots.com (the "Service"). This policy explains what personal information we collect, why we use it, who we share it with, how long we keep it, and the choices you have. It applies to customers and to visitors of our website. Sellers (Vendors) are also covered by their own agreement. Questions or requests: **privacy@desidots.com**.

## 1. What we collect
**Information you give us**

| Category | Examples | Why |
|---|---|---|
| Account identifiers | Name, email address and profile photo from your Google account (we never see your Google password) | To create and secure your account |
| Contact details | Phone number | To deliver orders and contact you about them |
| Profile details you choose to add | Gender, date of birth, community | To personalise events and offers. You can leave these out |
| Delivery addresses | Name, phone, street, city, state, ZIP code, delivery notes | To deliver your orders and show stores and events near you |
| Orders and tickets | What you bought, from which store, amounts, status, tracking details, cancellations and refunds | To run your orders and keep records |
| Reviews and reports | Ratings, comments, messages and screenshots you send us | To show reviews and help you |
| Contact form and emails | Your name, email address, optional order number and message | To answer you. The website form sends your message to our support mailbox |
| Seller information (Vendors only) | Business details, tax and identity details needed for payouts (for example EIN, PAN, GSTIN, import-export code) and bank details | To verify and pay sellers. Full bank account numbers are stored separately with extra protection |

**Collected automatically:** notification messages we create for your orders and payments (and whether you read them), and technical information needed to keep you signed in and keep the Service secure (such as a session token).

**What we do not collect:** your card number (Stripe collects it on its own secure page); your phone's GPS location (the app uses only the addresses you enter); advertising identifiers; and we use **no third-party advertising or analytics trackers**. The website sets no cookies.

## 2. How we use it
- To create your account, show stores and events, and take, deliver, cancel and refund your orders.
- To process payments, refunds and seller payouts.
- To send you messages about your orders, payments, refunds and, if you allow, new events in your city.
- To prevent fraud, enforce our Terms and keep the Service secure.
- To meet legal, tax and accounting duties, and to answer support requests.
- To improve the Service, using information in a way that does not identify you where we can.

## 3. Who we share it with
We do **not sell** your personal information, and we do **not share** it for cross-context behavioural advertising.

| Who | What and why |
|---|---|
| **The Vendor you order from** | The items ordered and your delivery name, address and phone number, so they can ship your order. Not your email or payment details. Vendors may use it only to fulfil your order. |
| **Stripe** (payments) | Payment details you enter on Stripe's page, your email and the order amount, to take payments and make refunds. Stripe's own privacy policy applies. |
| **Supabase** (hosting and database) | Stores the data described in this policy for us. |
| **Zoho** (email) | Handles the email sent to and from our support and privacy mailboxes, including messages from the website contact form. |
| **Vercel** (website hosting) | Hosts www.desidots.com and runs the contact form. |
| **Meta (WhatsApp)** | For sellers who choose it: the seller's WhatsApp number and the order number and store name in the alert we send about a new order. |
| **Google** | Sign-in. |
| **Geoapify** | The address text you type, to suggest and check addresses. |
| **Carriers and tracking pages** | When you open a tracking link you go to the carrier's (or 17TRACK's) website. They have their own policies. |
| **Our advisers** | Accountants and lawyers, under confidentiality. |
| **Authorities and others, where required** | If the law requires it, to respond to legal process, to protect rights, safety and property, or in a sale or reorganisation of the business (the buyer must honour this policy). |

We may share de-identified information that cannot reasonably be linked to you.

## 4. How long we keep it
- **Account information:** until you delete your account (section 7).
- **Orders, payments and refunds:** **7 years** after the order, as tax, accounting and dispute rules require. When you delete your account these records are kept **without your name, phone number or street address** (only the city and state remain) and are no longer linked to you.
- **Reviews:** deleted with your account.
- **Support screenshots and profile photos:** deleted with your account.
- **Seller records:** while the seller account is active and for as long as tax and legal rules require afterwards.
- **Backups** may keep deleted data for a short time before it is overwritten.

## 5. Security
Data travels over encrypted connections. Access is limited by account and role: customers can see only their own data, and Vendors only the orders placed with them. Sensitive seller bank details are stored apart from other data, and every time an administrator views a full account number it is logged. No system is perfectly secure. If a breach affects you, we will tell you and the authorities as the law requires.

## 6. Your rights and choices
Depending on where you live (for example California, Virginia, Colorado, Connecticut, Utah, Texas and other states), you may have the right to:
- **know** what personal information we collect, use and disclose, and **access** a copy;
- **correct** inaccurate information;
- **delete** your information (with the exceptions in section 4);
- **opt out of sale or sharing** for advertising: we do neither, so there is nothing to opt out of;
- **not be discriminated against** for using your rights.

**How to use them.** Change your profile and addresses yourself in the app (Profile). To use any other right, email **privacy@desidots.com** from your account email, or use the in-app deletion option. We may ask for details to confirm it is you. We reply within **45 days**; if we need more time we will tell you. If we refuse a request you may appeal by replying to our email within 60 days, and we will answer in writing. You may use an authorised agent with your written permission. We do not charge for requests unless they are clearly excessive.

**Notifications.** You can turn notifications off in Profile. We will still send messages needed for your orders and account.

**California.** In the last 12 months we have collected the categories listed in section 1 (identifiers, contact and address details, commercial records of purchases, profile details you add, and content you send us) for the purposes in section 2, and disclosed them to the recipients in section 3. We have not sold or shared personal information for advertising. We do not knowingly collect sensitive personal information beyond your account login and, if you enter it, a date of birth and gender; we use it only to personalise events and offers. We do not respond to "Do Not Track" signals because we do not track you across sites.

## 7. Deleting your account
In the app: **Profile, then "Delete my account"**. Your account locks immediately and you are signed out everywhere. It is deleted for good after **30 days**, and you can keep it by signing in again before then. You cannot delete while an order is on its way or a refund is still being paid. Details: [Delete your account](/delete-account/). You can also email privacy@desidots.com.

## 8. Children
The Service is for people **18 and older**. We do not knowingly collect information from anyone under 18. If you think a child has given us information, email privacy@desidots.com and we will delete it.

## 9. Where data is processed
Our service providers may process data in the United States and other countries. For orders from India, your delivery name, address and phone number are shared with the seller in India so they can ship to you.

## 10. Other sites
The Service links to carrier pages and other websites we do not control. Their privacy practices are their own.

## 11. Changes to this policy
We may update this policy. The effective date at the top shows the latest version. For important changes we will tell you in the app or by email before they take effect.
