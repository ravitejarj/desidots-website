# Vendor Agreement

**DRAFT: have a lawyer review before using.**
Effective date: [EFFECTIVE DATE]

This agreement is between **Groccy Technologies, Inc., doing business as DesiDots** ("DesiDots") and the business named on the vendor account ("Vendor", "you"). By being activated as a Vendor you agree to it, together with the Customer [Terms and Conditions](/terms/), [Refund and Cancellation Policy](/refunds/) and [Shipping Policy](/shipping/).

## 1. Your role
You are an **independent seller**, not an employee, partner or agent of DesiDots. You sell products to customers through DesiDots. DesiDots provides the marketplace, collects the customer's payment, and pays you as set out below. You are responsible for your products and your taxes.

## 2. Onboarding
- DesiDots creates and activates your account and stores. We may ask for identity and business documents (for USA: EIN; for India: PAN, GSTIN where required, and an import-export code (IEC) where needed) and a bank account for payouts.
- Your account must be **active** for customers to order. We may set it to pending or suspended if documents are incomplete, there are repeated problems, or we suspect fraud.
- DesiDots adds and edits your products on your behalf [DECIDE: currently vendors do not edit products themselves; update when that changes]. You give us accurate product information, photos, prices and stock counts.

## 3. Your products and promises
You promise that:
- the products are yours to sell, are **legal**, safe and accurately described (including weight, size, ingredients and any food or health claims);
- you comply with all laws that apply to what you sell and ship (including food, labelling, export and import rules, and product safety);
- you will not sell prohibited items: [DECIDE a list: illegal goods, weapons, counterfeits, hazardous goods, items that cannot lawfully be shipped];
- you keep your **stock counts** accurate; if you cannot supply an item you report it **immediately** in the app (see section 6).

## 4. Prices and money
**USA vendors.** You set the price customers pay. DesiDots keeps a **commission of [DECIDE: 8]%** of the item total. You are paid the rest. Shipping and the card processing fee paid by the customer are not part of your payout [DECIDE: confirm].

**India vendors.** You set your price in **Indian rupees**. Customers see a US-dollar price that DesiDots calculates (exchange rate, markup and rounding). **You are paid exactly your rupee price** for what ships, whatever the exchange rate does afterwards. The difference between what the customer pays and your rupee price is DesiDots' margin. [DECIDE: confirm shipping: currently the flat shipping fee the customer pays stays with DesiDots and you ship at your own cost, or part of it is paid to you.]

**Refunds and cancellations.** If an order is cancelled before shipping, or items are refunded, you are not paid for those items. If a customer is refunded after you have been paid, DesiDots may deduct the amount from your next payout.

## 5. Shipping
- Ship paid orders within **[DECIDE: 2 business days]**, using a trackable carrier.
- In the app, enter the **carrier and tracking number**, and add photos of the packed parcel and a short note if useful.
- Pack so items arrive safely. Include nothing prohibited. You are responsible for any customs paperwork for international parcels.
- Do not ship an order that was cancelled. The app tells you when a customer cancels.

## 6. Missing items
If you cannot supply some or all items of a paid order, mark them **not available** in the app straight away.
- If **everything** is missing, the order is cancelled and refunded.
- If **some** items are missing, the customer chooses to ship the rest or cancel. If they do not reply in **24 hours** the rest is shipped and the missing items refunded. You may ship only after the customer decides.
Repeated missing items, late shipping or poor service can lead to suspension.

## 7. Payouts
- You are paid by **bank wire** to the verified account on your profile.
- An order becomes payable when it is **delivered**, or **7 days after it shipped**, whichever comes first, as long as it has no refund, dispute or review flag. [DECIDE: confirm the 7 days.]
- The **minimum payout** is **$25**. Amounts below it roll into the next payout.
- Wire and bank fees: [DECIDE: DesiDots pays / you pay]. For India vendors the dollar amount sent is based on the exchange rate on the day of the wire, to cover your rupee amount.
- You are responsible for your own taxes. DesiDots may report payments as the law requires and may withhold payments where the law requires or where we need identity or tax details.

## 8. Customers and data
You may use customers' names, addresses and phone numbers **only to deliver their order**. Do not contact them for marketing or sell or share their information. Delete it when you no longer need it for the order and your records.

## 9. Quality, reviews and complaints
Customers can rate your store after delivery. You must respond to support questions promptly. Fake reviews, asking customers to change reviews, or contacting customers outside the order for other purposes is not allowed.

## 10. Brand and content
You allow DesiDots to show your store name, logo, photos and descriptions in the Service and in promotion of DesiDots. You keep ownership of your content.

## 11. Liability
You are responsible for claims about your products, your shipping and your compliance with law, and agree to **cover DesiDots' reasonable losses** from them [DECIDE and have a lawyer set limits]. DesiDots is not liable for lost profits or indirect loss, and our total liability to you is limited to the payouts due to you in the previous [DECIDE: 3] months, to the fullest extent the law allows.

## 12. Ending
Either side may end this agreement with **[DECIDE: 14]** days' notice. DesiDots may suspend or end it at once for serious breaches or risk. Orders already placed must be finished or refunded. We pay what is properly due for completed orders.
