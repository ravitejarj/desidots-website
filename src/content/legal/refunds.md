# Refund and Cancellation Policy

**DRAFT: have a lawyer review before publishing.**
Effective date: November 2, 2026

## 1. Cancelling an order yourself
You can cancel an order in the app (Orders, open the order, "Cancel order") **until the Vendor ships it**. Once it has shipped it can no longer be cancelled in the app: see section 4.

| Situation | What you get back |
|---|---|
| You cancel an order you have **not paid for yet** | Nothing was charged, so nothing to refund. The payment page is closed. |
| You cancel a **paid** order before it ships | The price of the items **and shipping**. The **processing fee** (2.9% + $0.30) is **not refunded**, because it is paid to our payment provider and cannot be recovered. |
| Event tickets | Not cancellable in the app. Contact support@desidots.com. [DECIDE: suggested rule, "refund if requested at least 7 days before the event"]. |

## 2. When the Vendor cannot supply your order
- **The whole order cannot be supplied:** the order is cancelled and you receive a **full refund, including the processing fee**.
- **Only some items cannot be supplied:** we tell you in the app. You choose:
  1. **Ship the rest.** You are refunded the price of the missing items. Shipping and the processing fee stay, because the rest of your order is still shipped.
  2. **Cancel the whole order.** You are refunded **everything, including the processing fee.**
  If you do not answer within **24 hours**, we ship the rest and refund the missing items.

## 3. Payments that arrive late
If you pay after an order was cancelled (for example you left the payment page open), we refund the **whole payment automatically**, including the fee, and tell you in the app.

## 4. Orders that have shipped
You cannot cancel in the app after shipping. Contact support@desidots.com if:
- the order has not arrived [DECIDE: suggested, "after the estimated delivery window plus 7 days"], or
- it arrived damaged, or it is not what you ordered.

Please send your order number and, for damage or wrong items, clear photos within **[DECIDE: 7]** days of delivery. We will work with the Vendor and, where the problem is theirs, arrange a replacement or a refund. [DECIDE: change if some products (for example perishable food) are not returnable.]

Use the "Report an issue" option in the app, or email us.

## 5. How and when refunds are paid
- Refunds go **back to the card you paid with**.
- We send the refund promptly. Your bank can take **5 to 10 business days** to show it.
- We will send you a notification when the refund is sent.

## 6. Prices in rupees
India-store prices are shown in US dollars at the price you saw when you ordered. Refunds are in US dollars, for the amount you paid. We are not responsible for exchange-rate changes between your bank and the US dollar.

## 7. Customs and duties
If an import authority returns, destroys or holds an India order, or charges duties or fees, see our [Shipping Policy](/shipping/). Refunds in those cases depend on the carrier's and customs' findings.

## 8. Abuse
We may refuse refunds or close accounts where we find repeated claims that are untrue or abuse of this policy.
