# Refund and Cancellation Policy

Effective date: November 2, 2026

This policy is part of our [Terms and Conditions](/terms/). It explains when you can cancel, when you get money back, and how to report a problem. Please contact us **before** disputing a charge with your bank: we can usually fix things faster. Email support@desidots.com or use "Need help with this order?" on your order page. We reply within 2 business days.

## 1. Cancelling an order yourself
You can cancel an order in the app (Orders, open the order, "Cancel order") **until the Vendor ships it**. After it has shipped it can no longer be cancelled in the app (see section 5).

| Situation | What you get back |
|---|---|
| You cancel an order you have **not paid** for | Nothing was charged. The payment page is closed. |
| You cancel a **paid** order before it ships | The price of the items **and shipping**. The **processing fee** (2.9% + $0.30) is **not refunded**, because it is paid to our payment provider and cannot be recovered. |
| Event tickets | See section 7. |

## 2. When a Vendor cannot supply your order
- **The whole order cannot be supplied.** The order is cancelled and you receive a **full refund, including the processing fee**.
- **Only some items cannot be supplied.** We tell you in the app. You choose:
  1. **Ship the rest.** You are refunded the price of the missing items. Shipping and the processing fee stay, because the rest of your order is still shipped.
  2. **Cancel the whole order.** You are refunded **everything, including the processing fee.**

  If you do not answer within **24 hours**, we ship the rest and refund the missing items. You can cancel the whole order at any time before that.

## 3. Payments that arrive late
If you pay after an order was cancelled (for example you left the payment page open and paid after the time ran out), we refund the **whole payment automatically**, including the fee, and tell you in the app.

## 4. Problems with an order that has shipped
Report every problem in writing to support@desidots.com with your **order number**. Keep the packaging. The time limits below keep claims fair and let us check with the Vendor and the carrier.

| Problem | Report it within | What to send | What we do |
|---|---|---|---|
| **Not received** (USA stores) | 7 days after the estimated delivery date, and no later than 45 days after the order was shipped | Order number | We check tracking and ask the Vendor and carrier. If it is lost or cannot be found, the Vendor reships or you are refunded. |
| **Not received** (India stores) | 14 days after the estimated delivery date, and no later than 60 days after the order was shipped | Order number | Same. Parcels held or returned by customs are covered in section 6. |
| **Damaged, spoiled, wrong or defective item** | 7 days after delivery. **Food and other perishable items: 48 hours** | Clear photos of the item, the packaging and the shipping label | A replacement, or a refund of the affected item. If the mistake was the Vendor's, shipping for the item is refunded too. |
| **Missing item** from a delivered parcel | 7 days after delivery | Photos of the parcel and contents | The missing item is sent or refunded. |

A claim made after these times, or without the details above, may be declined. We decide claims fairly using tracking, photos, and information from the Vendor and the carrier.

**Changing your mind after shipping.** Orders cannot be returned or refunded only because you changed your mind, unless the Vendor's own page says it accepts returns. **Perishable food, opened items, personalised items and items made to order cannot be returned.** If a return is agreed in writing, it must be approved by us first, and the return shipping cost is yours unless the item was wrong or damaged.

## 5. What is not refunded
- The **card processing fee**, except where section 2 or 3 says it is refunded, or an event is cancelled.
- **Shipping** once an order has shipped, unless the item was lost, damaged or wrong because of the Vendor or carrier.
- **Import duties, customs fees and taxes** you paid to authorities or carriers.
- Items you refuse or fail to collect, or that are returned because of a wrong or incomplete address.
- Orders where we find the claim to be untrue, or the item was used, damaged by you, or misdescribed by you.

## 6. Orders from India: customs
Parcels from India pass through US customs. Customs may inspect, delay, hold, return or destroy a parcel. If a parcel is **held or returned by customs because the item is restricted or the paperwork was wrong on the Vendor's side**, we work with the Vendor to refund or reship the order. If it is held or refused for a reason connected to you (for example you refuse to pay duties or the address is wrong), the order is not refunded. See the [Shipping Policy](/shipping/).

## 7. Event tickets
- **You** can ask for a refund up to **7 days before the event starts**. You get the ticket price back. The processing fee is not refunded.
- **Less than 7 days before the event, or after it starts**, tickets are not refunded, unless the event is cancelled.
- **If the organiser cancels the event**, you are refunded the ticket price **and** the processing fee. If the event is postponed, your ticket normally stays valid for the new date; if you cannot attend the new date, contact us within 14 days of the announcement.
- Tickets cannot be cancelled in the app. Email support@desidots.com with your order number.

## 8. How and when refunds are paid
- Refunds go **back to the card you paid with**. We cannot refund to a different card or account.
- We send approved refunds promptly. Your bank can take **5 to 10 business days** to show the money.
- We send you a notification when a refund is sent.
- Refunds are in US dollars, for the amount you paid. We are not responsible for exchange rates or fees charged by your bank.

## 9. Chargebacks and abuse
Please contact us first. If you instead dispute a charge with your bank, we give the bank our records. If a charge you disputed was valid, or you were already refunded, we may close your account and recover the amount. We may refuse refunds and close accounts where we find repeated untrue claims or abuse of this policy.
