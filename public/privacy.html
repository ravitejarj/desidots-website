Privacy Policy

Last updated: October 7, 2026

DesiDots ("we," "us," "our") operates the DesiDots mobile app and website, a marketplace for South Asian cultural events, groceries shipped from India, and fashion. This policy explains what information we collect, how we use it, and the choices you have.

1. Information we collect

  • Account information: name, email address, phone number, and profile photo when you create an account.
  • Order & booking information: event tickets purchased, store orders placed, shipping/delivery details, and order history.
  • Payment information: processed directly by our payment provider, Stripe. We do not store your full card number on our servers.
  • Location information: approximate location, if you grant permission, used to show nearby events.
  • Usage information: how you interact with the app, for troubleshooting and improving the product.

2. How we use your information

  • To create and manage your account
  • To process ticket purchases and store orders, and notify vendors or hosts when you place one
  • To send order, shipping, and event updates via push notification, email, or (where enabled) WhatsApp
  • To show you relevant events and vendors near you
  • To maintain the security and integrity of the platform

3. Third-party services we use

We rely on the following services to operate DesiDots. Each only receives the information necessary to perform its function:

  • Supabase — database, authentication, and backend infrastructure
  • Stripe — payment processing for ticket purchases
  • Resend — transactional email delivery
  • Twilio — WhatsApp order notifications (where enabled)
  • Expo — push notification delivery

4. Vendors and hosts on the platform

When you place an order with a store or book an event, we share the information necessary to fulfill that order (such as your name, contact details, and order contents) with the relevant vendor or event host. Vendors and hosts are independent third parties responsible for their own products, events, and fulfillment — see our Terms of Service for details.

5. Your rights

You can access, update, or delete your account information at any time from within the app's Settings. To request a full export or deletion of your data, contact us using the details below.

6. Children's privacy

DesiDots is not directed at children under 13, and we do not knowingly collect personal information from children under 13.

7. Data retention

We retain your information for as long as your account is active or as needed to provide the service, comply with legal obligations, resolve disputes, and enforce our agreements.

8. Changes to this policy

We may update this policy from time to time. We'll notify you of material changes through the app or by email.

Contact us

Questions about this policy or your data? Email us at privacy@desidots.com.
